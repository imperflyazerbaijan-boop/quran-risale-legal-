Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy describes how Quran Risale AI Studio (the “App”) processes information when an account holder chooses to connect Instagram or another supported social-media account. The App is in development; not all described functionality is active.

1. Controller and contact

The operator responsible for the App is OPERATOR_NAME_HERE. For privacy questions and requests, contact CONTACT_EMAIL_HERE.

2. Information processed

Depending on functionality enabled and permissions you expressly grant, the App may process:

  • Platform account identifiers, profile/username and connected-account metadata;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens and granted permission details used to authorize actions;
  • Videos, images, captions, schedules, playlist identifiers and publishing metadata you choose to prepare or submit;
  • Local status records, errors and technical logs necessary for troubleshooting and preventing duplicate publication.

The App does not need your Instagram password; account authorization is performed through the platform's own login and consent flow.

3. Purposes and legal basis

The App uses connected-account information to identify the account you selected, maintain authorized connections, prepare content, publish only as authorized, display operation status, and protect against accidental posting to the wrong account. Where GDPR applies, processing required to provide a service requested by the account holder may rely on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; security and diagnostic processing may rely on legitimate interests under Art. 6(1)(f), where applicable. Optional processing is subject to any additional consent required by law. The actual legal basis depends on the deployment and relationship to the user.

4. Storage and sharing

The current application is designed primarily for local operation, with configuration and OAuth credentials stored on the operator's own device. When enabled, account connection and publication necessarily involve communications with Meta/Instagram and other selected platforms, which process information under their own privacy policies. Publishing an Instagram Reel may require making an authorized video temporarily available at a publicly reachable HTTPS location; the operator must document any hosting provider used before enabling that feature. The App does not sell personal information.

5. Retention and security

Account authorizations and related local records are intended to be kept only while necessary to provide the requested account connection, publication workflow or relevant security diagnostics. Reasonable access controls should be applied to tokens and configuration files. No system can guarantee absolute security. If storage, backup or hosting arrangements change, this policy should be updated accordingly.

You can withdraw third-party authorization through the relevant platform's Apps and Websites/settings controls, and may request disconnection or deletion of App-held account information by contacting CONTACT_EMAIL_HERE. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, restriction, portability or deletion of personal data and object to certain processing. See Data Deletion Instructions.

7. Children's data

The App is intended for use by persons legally entitled to authorize their own social-media accounts. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as the App's functionality, hosting and legal requirements change. The date above will be revised when material changes are published.
