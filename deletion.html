Data Deletion Instructions

Effective / last updated: 29 October 2026

If you connected an Instagram account to Quran Risale AI Studio, you can revoke permission and request deletion of information held by the App.

1. Revoke platform access

Open Instagram settings, find the website/app permissions or connected apps section, and remove Quran Risale AI Studio if listed. The exact menu labels may differ by device and version. This stops future API access using the revoked authorization, but it does not by itself guarantee removal of copies previously stored by the App.

2. Request deletion from the App

Email CONTACT_EMAIL_HERE with the subject Quran Risale AI Studio — Data Deletion Request. Include the Instagram username associated with the account and say whether you want the connection removed or all App-held data associated with that account deleted. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens or identity documents in the first message.

3. What happens next

The operator will verify ownership or authorization by an appropriate, proportionate means and process the request in accordance with applicable law, deleting locally retained authorization tokens and related account information where no lawful retention requirement applies. The operator will confirm the outcome or explain any lawful limitation by email. If a right under GDPR applies, the operator will respond within the applicable statutory period.

4. Published content and platform-held data

Deleting App-held data does not automatically delete videos or account information already stored by Instagram or other independent platforms. Remove published posts through the relevant platform and refer to its privacy and deletion procedures for platform-held information.
